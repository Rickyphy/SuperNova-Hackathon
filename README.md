# 🌊 FloodRecon: AI-Powered Flood Hazard Detection System

> *"From Sky to Safety — Real-time Disaster Intelligence Through Drone Vision"*

---

## 🚀 The Vision

Every year, floods devastate communities, destroy infrastructure, and claim countless lives. The critical window for rescue and damage assessment is measured in hours, not days. Traditional methods rely on ground surveys that are slow, dangerous, and often impossible in flooded regions.

**AeroGuard changes that.**

Our system deploys drones to capture aerial footage of flooded regions and uses cutting-edge AI to instantly identify life-threatening hazards — collapsed electric poles, dangerous water logging, structural cracks, and compromised buildings. Within minutes, emergency responders receive a color-coded hazard map that directs them to where help is needed most.

**We're not just building a tool. We're building a lifeline.**
